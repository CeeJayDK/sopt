What shader math costs, per GPU architecture.

The cost models SweetOpt uses to find faster shader code, measured on real cards with GPU Blueprint's OpBench. The goal: every architecture that runs DirectX 11.

1.0 = one fma (a fused multiply-add at full rate), measured as throughput: many independent operations in parallel. Lower is better. Numbers are rounded to what the cost models use. Texture costs are on the TexBench page.
