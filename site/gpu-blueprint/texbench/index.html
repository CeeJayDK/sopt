What textures cost, and how GPUs order their pixels.

Texture reads in every ReShade format and filter, lookup tables, caches, render target writes, blending and pass states, measured on real cards with GPU Blueprint's TexBench.

Costs are in fma units (1.0 = one fused multiply-add at full rate) on top of the shader math around each read, measured as throughput, so they compare directly with the cost models. Writes are in GB/s (higher is better), blending and pass states in ms per full-screen pass at 3840 x 2160.
